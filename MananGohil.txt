Mission Overview:
Cansat = payload + container ⇒ mount on top of the rocket.

payload ⇒ inside the container. Nose cone is part of the payload.

Triggers:
At peak altitude ⇒ Container separates from the rocket ( by means of rocket ejection motor.)
		Container shall descent at a rate of <= 20m/s by means of a parachute.
at 75% of peak altitude ⇒ Payload separates from the container. Payload starts descending with rate = 5m/s. Auto-gyro is used to stabilize the cansat.

(2 video cameras show the separation of the payload from the container (i.e at 75% peak altitude) ⇒ auto-gyro functioning is also monitored by this camera so most preferably it is on the inside of the payload.
Second video camera to be oriented at an angle of 45deg (towards north) and it needs to be spin stabilized.)

Data is collected and transmitted at 1Hz (sampling rate can be much higher. but GCS should display at 1Hz only)

Telemetry to be considered : Interior Temp, battery voltage, altitude, auto-gyro rotation rate, accelaration rate, magnetic field and GPS Position.
